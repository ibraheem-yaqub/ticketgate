The endpoint crate

Nine routes.One meter.

Pricing

Three tiers of toll.No plans.

Illustrative per-call prices by route family. Final prices are set at launch and always shown in the 402 quote before your agent pays.

Price$0.001

Last, bid, ask, spread. Batch up to 20 symbols for one toll.

Depth$0.003

Order book levels and top-of-book imbalance.

History$0.01

Candles, recent fills and VWAP over a window.

NoteAll prices on this page are illustrative and may change before launch. Symbols such as STK·A are sample placeholders, not real listings.
Conventions

Same gate,every route.

Base URL

api.tickgate.live

HTTPS only, JSON responses. Not live yet: pre-launch.

Auth

Payment is the key

No API keys. A paid quote plus the X-Payment header unlocks one response.

Numbers

Prices as strings

Decimal strings avoid float rounding. Sizes are integers.
